Abhishek Nayak, PhD

Senior Software Engineer · MathWorks

I'm a Senior Software Engineer in Test at MathWorks, working on sensor simulation for autonomous systems.

I hold a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Texas A&M University. My research covered motion planning, perception, and machine learning for self-driving vehicles, UAVs, and indoor robots, including multi-agent path planning, computer vision for ADAS, and infrastructure-enabled autonomy. It has produced 10+ publications and 120+ citations.
